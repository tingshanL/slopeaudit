Beat the Blues contributed 100 participants and 380 observed BDI-II scores across months 0, 2, 3, 5, and 8. The participant slope was defined as the best linear individual deviation from the treatment-specific mean trajectory over months 0–8, not as each participant's literal linear disease course or an individual causal treatment effect. Under homogeneous independent residuals, the repaired heterogeneity statistic was LR=4.07184 (boundary-bootstrap p=0.0772317, Monte Carlo interval [0.0605879, 0.0945789]).
