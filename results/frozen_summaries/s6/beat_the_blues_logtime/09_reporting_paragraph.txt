The frozen Beat the Blues hierarchy remains unchanged: the a priori homogeneous model and the planned occasion-specific-variance sensitivity did not establish patient-specific slope heterogeneity. As an explicitly post-hoc estimand check, we replaced centered linear month only in the random-effects design with a centered and scaled log(1+month) score; treatment-by-occasion fixed means, observed schedules, and occasion-specific residual variances were unchanged. The log-time RI-versus-RI+RS statistic was LR=5.89787; the observed-schedule boundary bootstrap gave p=0.034034 with Monte Carlo interval [0.0228683, 0.0461256]. The Question 1 result is time-estimand-sensitive: the post-hoc log-time slope produced exploratory support, but it cannot overturn the frozen unsupported primary hierarchy. Questions 2 and 3 remain conditional fitted-model descriptions rather than evidence for an established symptom-change trait.
